## 1. Short Homepage / Product Section Draft

### AI conversations with clearer controls.

Most AI chat and translation tools ask your team to trade away visibility for convenience. You paste sensitive notes into a black box, wonder which model or billing pool is actually running, and hope a stray browser tab isn't still listening to your microphone or recording your screen.

**WorksAI Live Interpreter** (`https://translate.worksai.org`) is built for small B2B operational teams that want practical AI assistance without losing track of credentials, budgets, or consent:

- **One workspace, three distinct modes**: Switch deliberately between **Chat** (GPT and Gemini via Hostinger AI Router), **Transcribe recording** (Cohere audio-to-text and structured follow-ups), and **Live interpretation** (OpenAI real-time speech-to-speech when a direct OpenAI key is configured). Audio never crosses into a mode or provider you didn't select.
- **Keys stay on the server**: Generation credentials live in server-side Cloudflare Worker bindings or tenant Bring-Your-Own-Key (BYOK) records encrypted with AES-256-GCM using a dedicated `BYOK_ENCRYPTION_KEY` separate from session signing secrets. Browser clients only ever see masked fingerprints.
- **Explicit microphone and provider consent**: Recording and live audio require an explicit user click and consent check before capture begins, and stopping a recording releases browser media tracks immediately.
- **Predictable usage caps and honest fallback**: Shared per-user, per-tenant, and platform rate limits (with a 400-token output cap) are reserved in Cloudflare R2 before a provider call starts. Cross-provider fallback from GPT to Gemini happens only when you opt in and never rotates keys to bypass rate limits or billing errors.
- **Invite-gated Google sign-in**: Team members can sign in with Google (`openid`, `email`, `profile`), verified server-side against Google's OIDC keys and gated by operator-issued workspace invites—with no automatic email-based account merging and no coupling between login identity and model API keys.
- **Zero session-replay analytics in your workspace**: Microsoft Clarity is blocked by Content Security Policy and runtime checks on authenticated workspace routes (`/`), and runs on the public `/demo` shell only after explicit consent and DOM masking verification.

## 2. Concise Release Announcement Draft

**Title**: WorksAI Live Interpreter v1.3.1: Simpler Chat, Unified GPT & Gemini Routing, Invite-Gated Google Sign-In, and Clearer Controls

We've shipped **WorksAI Live Interpreter v1.3.1** at [translate.worksai.org](https://translate.worksai.org) ([Public Demo](https://translate.worksai.org/demo)). This release strips away interface clutter while tightening how credentials, audio consent, and quotas work under the hood.

What's new in v1.3.1:
- **Streamlined Deep Chat workspace**: One conversation view, one composer, one attachment button, one microphone control, and one compact mode/model selector (`Chat` · `Transcribe recording` · `Live interpretation`; `GPT` · `Gemini`). Provider routing, BYOK, regional targets, retention, and quota ledgers now live cleanly inside `Settings ⚙`.
- **Unified Hostinger AI Router adapter (`gpt-5-mini` & `gemini-3.5-flash-lite`)**: Both GPT and Gemini chat routes share a single server-side Hostinger AI Router credential reference (platform credits or admin-managed tenant BYOK). Gemini supports bounded audio attachments (`WAV`, `MP3`, `OGG`, `WEBM`, `FLAC` up to 60 seconds / 5 MiB) for either transcription or Q&A, while GPT chat rejects audio uploads upfront with a one-click prompt to switch modes.
- **Invite-gated Sign in with Google**: Server-side Google OIDC ID token verification (`RS256` signature, issuer, audience, expiry, nonce, and verified email) paired with operator invite codes for first-time account linking.
- **CLI Doctor & GitHub CI/CD**: Run `npm run doctor` (`npm run setup:keys`) to inspect credential provenance and migrate misplaced keys without echoing secrets to your terminal history.
- **Tested rollback path**: Includes a prebuilt zero-inference maintenance Worker (`worksai-live-interpreter-maintenance-worker.mjs`) that keeps `translate.worksai.org` attached while halting all paid provider calls if an incident occurs.

Try the zero-cost synthetic sandbox at [translate.worksai.org/demo](https://translate.worksai.org/demo) or clone the repository directly from the **Open in your IDE** menu.

## 4. Five Derivative Content Angles (`@generate-fresh-ideas` Lenses)

### Remix: WorksAI Live Interpreter v1.3.1 — “AI conversations with clearer controls”

🔥 **CONTRARIAN**: Why "automatic AI failover" is usually a billing and privacy bug, not a feature.
   → Most AI gateways brag about silently routing your failed prompt across five different model vendors until one answers. For operational teams, silent failover means a prompt meant for OpenAI quietly lands on another vendor's servers—or burns through a second API key after your primary budget hits 429. We built WorksAI Live Interpreter so fallback from GPT to Gemini only happens when you explicitly check the consent box, stays inside the same regional target, and stops dead on 401, 402, or 429.

📖 **PERSONAL STORY**: The 20-button AI dashboard we had to throw out so people could actually have a conversation.
   → When we wired up real-time translation, Cohere audio transcription, and multi-provider chat, our first interface looked like an airplane cockpit—separate panels, competing mic buttons, and raw quota counters everywhere. Nobody wants to audit lease counters just to send a message or transcribe a 30-second voice note. Here is how we collapsed the entire workspace down to one chat stage, one composer, and one Settings drawer without giving up a single server-side safety gate.

📊 **DATA ANGLE**: What happens when you cap output at 400 tokens and reserve quota leases before calling the model.
   → Runaway AI bills rarely come from normal daily usage—they come from concurrent retries, unvalidated 20 MB audio uploads, and uncapped completions. By validating audio container headers and duration (`<= 60s`, `<= 5 MiB`) at the Cloudflare edge and reserving atomic R2 admission leases *before* touching Hostinger or Cohere, teams can keep shared evaluation budgets predictable (2 chat requests/minute per user, 20 platform attempts/day).

✅ **PLAYBOOK**: How to set up multi-provider AI keys in Cloudflare Workers without leaking secrets to your shell history.
   → A 5-minute tactical checklist for operators: (1) Run `npm run doctor` to inspect missing bindings and catch misrouted gateway keys (`sk-hstgr-` vs direct `sk-`), (2) Never pass keys in CLI flags (`--api-key=...` is blocked with `CLI_ARGV_SECRET_FORBIDDEN`), (3) Pipe secrets via non-echoing `npx wrangler secret put`, and (4) Keep `BYOK_ENCRYPTION_KEY` separate from `JWT_SECRET`.

🎯 **WHAT THEY MISSED**: Why your "Sign in with Google" button shouldn't auto-merge accounts by email address.
   → Tutorials for adding Google OAuth almost always look up the user by `email` and link the account automatically. In a multi-tenant B2B workspace, blind email merging can let an uninvited external identity attach to a pre-staged workspace record—or mix up login tokens with Google Cloud/Gemini API keys. Keying accounts strictly by Google's immutable `sub` identifier and requiring a one-time operator invite code (`wai_inv_...`) for first-time linking closes that gap cleanly.
